Our Mission

Spreading literacy, one review at a time.

Why We Do It

Between the Lines was founded on the belief that everyone should have access to great stories. We noticed that many people have books gathering dust on their shelves, while others lack access to libraries.

By building a community of reviewers, we create a hub for book lovers to connect and, more importantly, a platform to facilitate book donations to schools, shelters, and community centers.
